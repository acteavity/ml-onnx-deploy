PyTorch models running in your browser with ONNX

Two tabular models trained in PyTorch, exported to ONNX and executed client-side with onnxruntime-web. Pre-processing (imputation, feature transform, standardization) is embedded in each ONNX graph, so the pages send raw values straight to the model.

Regression

Concrete compressive strength

8 mix-design inputs (kg/m³, age in days) → predicted strength in MPa.

Classification

Diabetes risk (Pima)

8 clinical measurements → probability of diabetes.
